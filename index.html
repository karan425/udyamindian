DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Documents Required for Udyam Registration

Keep your business details and relevant documents ready before starting the registration process.

♙

Proprietorship

For individually owned businesses

  • Owner's AadhaarFor identity authentication.
  • PAN DetailsPAN details of the proprietor.
  • Bank Account DetailsAccount number and IFSC code, as applicable.
♧

Partnership Firm

For businesses operated by partners

  • Aadhaar and PAN detailsDetails of the relevant partner.
  • Partnership deedWhere applicable.
  • Firm's bank account details
▦

Private Limited Company / LLP

  • Company PAN details
  • GSTIN detailsIf registered under GST.
  • Aadhaar of an authorised signatory
  • Certificate of Incorporation or LLP agreement
◇

Other Eligible Entities

Trusts, societies, co-operatives and similar entities

  • Entity registration certificate
  • Aadhaar of the authorised representative
  • PAN details of the entity or representative
▤

Additional Documents Needed

  • Business Name and TypeChoose the business name and activity type, such as manufacturing, services or trading.
  • Registered Address and Contact InformationKeep the business address and correct contact details ready.
  • Business and Investment DetailsPrepare information about investment in plant, machinery or equipment, turnover and number of employees.
